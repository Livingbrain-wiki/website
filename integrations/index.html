Integrations

Planned

Works with the tools you already use.

Everything on this page is planned. Other companies' tools are named in plain text; we're not affiliated with them.

Code

Where the work happens.

  • GitHubThe Living Brain GitHub App keeps the wiki in your own git repo and proposes every change as a pull request. You own it
  • Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Claude DesktopAny agent that supports MCP. Coding agents

Chat

Where people ask.

  • SlackInvite it to channels. It answers in the thread, with the asker's own access.
  • DiscordAnswers in threads, with a /brain ask slash command. Role-gated channels map to permissions, so it only answers with what the asker's roles can see.
  • WhatsApp and TelegramlaterAfter Slack and Discord.

Mail

Forward what matters, or connect a mailbox.

  • Owlpost brain inboxsimplestForward, BCC or auto-forward mail to your brain's own address on Owlpost, our sister email service. No mailbox access needed.
  • Gmail, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, IMAPConnect a whole mailbox and calendar, read-only. It stays in your personal memory. How mail stays private

Imports

Bring your history.

  • ChatGPTImport your export with the livingbrain CLI, on your machine. Step-by-step guide
  • ClaudeClaude exports import the same way.

Monitoring and logs

Read-only, so it knows when prod is down.

  • GrafanaAnswers "is prod down?" with the current numbers and a link to the exact panel.
  • Loki, Elasticsearch, Datadog, CloudWatch, Cloudflare, SentryReads your logs and says what changed: when the errors started and which deploy came before.
  • OpenTelemetryExports its own metrics, traces and logs, so you can watch it in Grafana, Datadog or Honeycomb.

Models

Bring your own LLM, on every plan.

  • Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, LiteLLMOr any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including self-hosted ones. Choose a model per role.
  • DeepSeekCrew includes $3 of DeepSeek credit each month. Pricing

Sister ventures

Other Factory Zero ventures it is designed to work with.

  • ColonizerRuns bigger jobs in isolated microVMs and returns pull requests. From chat to pull request
  • OwlpostThe brain's email inbox, and the screening every message passes first.
  • SupportGeniusAnswers your customers, only from pages you published to them.
  • PromptDecodeScreens everything the brain reads for hidden text. Screening
