04 · VOICE

Do

  1. Say what it does in plain verbs. "It opens the issue." "It merges duplicates."
  2. Show the source. Cite messages, and label every mock "Illustration" or "Sample data".
  3. Write like a calm teammate. Short, specific, a little dry.

Don't

  1. Claim what isn't built. No customer logos, testimonials, user counts or uptime.
  2. Reach for hype. No "revolutionary", "magic" or "supercharge".
  3. Imply partnerships. Name other tools in plain text, never with their logos.
