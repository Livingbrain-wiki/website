Privacy

What this site collects, and why.

No cookies, no ads, no tracking. The only data this site sends anywhere is your email, if you join the waitlist. Draft. Legal review pending. Last updated 2026-10-05.

Who is responsible.

This site is run by Factory Zero, the venture behind Living Brain. Factory Zero is the controller of the data described here. Living Brain itself is in design: only this website exists.

Browsing the site.

  • Nothing you type is read by us. The pages are static files. We run no analytics, no advertising, no pixels, no session tracking and no cookies.
  • One value in your own browser. If you pick light or dark, that choice is stored as lb-theme in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes the request data any web server needs, such as your IP address and user agent, in order to return a page. We add no logging of our own.

Joining the waitlist.

The waitlist is not open yet, so nothing is collected today. The form says so if you try. Here is what it will do once it opens.

  • One field: your email address. That is all the form collects.
  • Why: to write once, when there's something to try. We will not sell it, trade it, or use it for advertising.
  • Consent. You give it by joining, and you can take it back at any time.
  • How long we keep it. Until you ask us to delete it, or until we close the waitlist. Then we delete it.
  • Where it goes. With JavaScript on, the form sends your address, tagged with the product name, to the waitlist service at api.livingbrain.wiki, which runs on Cratefield, a Factory Zero venture (who else touches it). With JavaScript off, your own email app sends it to hello@livingbrain.wiki.

Who else handles it.

The full list of companies and ventures that run or will run parts of Living Brain, with what is live and what is planned, is on Who else touches it. If paid plans ever exist, they are covered by the terms of use.

Your rights.

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and we stop using your email. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we will do it.

If this changes.

When the product exists, this notice will be updated before any new data is collected, and the date above will change. Anything new we collect gets a line here before we collect it.

Contact: hello@livingbrain.wiki
