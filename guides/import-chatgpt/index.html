Guide · Import

Import your ChatGPT history

Export your ChatGPT history from ChatGPT, then import it with the livingbrain CLI. The preview comes first; nothing uploads until you confirm.

Years of ChatGPT conversations hold decisions, plans and prompts that worked. Living Brain reads them and keeps what matters as Markdown sources and pages, each one citing the conversation it came from.

1. Export your data from ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on the web, click your profile picture, then Settings.
  2. Open Data controls, choose Export data, and confirm.
  3. OpenAI emails you a download link. It expires after 24 hours, so download the .zip soon.

The zip holds every conversation in conversations.json, a readable copy in chat.html, a few small files about your account and feedback, and the files and images you uploaded.

If you use ChatGPT through a company workspace, personal export may be switched off; ask your admin. OpenAI's steps can change. Their help centre has the current version.

2. Import it

livingbrain import chatgpt ~/Downloads/chatgpt-export.zip
  • It reads on your machine. The zip is read in memory and never unpacked to disk.
  • You see a preview first. Nothing uploads until you answer y.
  • You choose what goes in. Add --since, --until, --keyword or --id to keep only some conversations.
  • Only the live branch is kept. Edited or regenerated answers that were abandoned, and hidden system messages, are left out.
  • Secrets are redacted before anything leaves your machine.
  • Importing again is safe. The same content is stored once, so a newer export adds only what is new.

Claude's export works the same way, from Settings → Privacy → Export data: livingbrain import claude ~/Downloads/claude-export.zip.

3. Then delete the zip

The export contains everything you ever typed into ChatGPT. Once it is imported, delete the zip from your Downloads folder and empty the trash.

For every import option, see the import reference. For the other way to bring notes in, see Imports.
