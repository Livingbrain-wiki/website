# Workspaces and members

A workspace is one brain. It has its own pages, sources, members, settings and
usage. Nothing crosses from one workspace to another.

## Roles

Every member has one role:

| Role | What it can do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, and changes to who is an owner. A workspace always keeps at least one owner. |
| **Admin** | Invites and removes members, changes workspace settings (name and picture, models, budgets, limits), and reads the usage and audit views. |
| **Member** | Reads what its access allows, writes its own notes and sources, and asks questions. |
| **Viewer** | Reads what its access allows. Cannot write. |

Owners and admins see the audit log (see [Access, agents and audit](/guides/access-agents-audit/)).

## Inviting people

Admins invite people by email from **Settings → Members**. The invitee signs in
with that email and joins the workspace with the role you chose. Removing a
member signs them out of the workspace.

## Switching and picking a workspace

- In the web app, the workspace menu lists every workspace you belong to, and
  **New workspace** creates another. You are its owner.
- In the Mac app, the sidebar footer and the **Workspace** menu switch between
  every workspace you have signed in to on this Mac.
- When you approve a device with `livingbrain login` or the Mac app, the approval
  page has a workspace picker, so the token is for the workspace you pick.

## Name and picture

Owners and admins set the workspace name and picture under **Settings →
Workspace**. Everyone in the workspace sees them in the top bar and the switcher.
Pictures are PNG, JPEG or WebP, cropped to a square in the browser.

Members can set their own name and picture under their profile.
