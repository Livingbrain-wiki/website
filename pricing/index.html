Pricing

Planned pricing

Priced per workspace, not per seat.

Self-hosting is free for small teams. Teams and hosting are paid. Living Brain is built in Rust on Cloudflare's edge, so it's fast and very cheap to run, and the price reflects that.

Plans

Community

Self-hosted

$0per workspace

  • Up to 5 people
  • Runs on your own Cloudflare account with your own LLM key
  • All core features: MCP for coding agents, the brain in Slack and Discord, the living wiki, citations, the 3D graph, agent log aggregation, export
  • Storage: your own Cloudflare account, no limit from us

Teams

Self-hosted or hosted

From $5/mo per workspace

  • Team features: SSO, admin and audit log, the shared team prompt library, per-channel policies, and a white-label app on your own domain
  • Self-hosted license: $5/mo per workspace, unlimited people, your own storage
  • Hosted by us: $90/yr (two months free) or $9/mo per workspace, with your own LLM key. Up to 25 people included, 10 GB storage included

Crew

Hosted

$150/yr or $15/mo per workspace

  • Hosted by us, with $5 of DeepSeek credit included each month, or bring your own LLM
  • Up to 25 people included
  • Team features and the white-label app included
  • 25 GB storage included

Storage covers wiki pages, sources, search indexes and aggregated agent logs. Extra hosted storage: $0.25 per GB a month. Extra people: +$5/mo per 25 on hosted plans. Billed annually by default; monthly available. Every plan: Markdown export, cited answers, and access that follows your chat's permissions (Slack channels, Discord roles). No hosted plan is free.
