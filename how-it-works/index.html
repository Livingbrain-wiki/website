How it works

Listen. Write. Evolve.

Living Brain reads your team's conversations, writes what it learns as a Markdown wiki with sources, and keeps that wiki true. All of it is planned; the examples are sample data.

Speaks up

It answers before anyone has to dig.

Living Brain listens in the team chat channels you invite it to, in Slack or Discord. When a question already has an answer, it replies in the thread and links to the exact message the answer came from.

Three steps, end to end.

→ project: Billing v2

One night in the brain.

While the team is offline it reads what happened, tidies what it knows and hands work to agents. It also scans new research papers, news and releases about your stack, and notes how they could apply to your projects. By morning there's a digest waiting.

Learns your way

The learning layer notices how each person likes answers, short or detailed, in threads or DMs, and adapts week by week, so its answers improve every week. It learns from agent sessions too

You own it

Your brain is a folder of Markdown.

Read it, edit it, version it, move it. Your knowledge isn't locked in a black-box vector store. Planned: keep it in your own git repo, where the Living Brain GitHub App proposes every change as a pull request and edits you merge flow back in.
